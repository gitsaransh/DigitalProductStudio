# Ultimate Finance OS — Excel Template workbook (v1.0)
**SKU:** DPS-XLS-001 | **Category:** Excel Templates | **Status:** Draft

## Installation & Setup Instructions

1. **Download**: Download `Ultimate_Finance_OS_v1.0.xlsx` to your computer.
2. **Open**: Open in Microsoft Excel (desktop app or Excel 365 web version).
3. **Configure**: Navigate to the `Setup` tab, select your currency symbol ($ , €, £, ¥), and custom name your income/expense categories.
4. **Transactions Log**: Navigate to the `Transactions` tab to log your MTD expenses.
5. **Dashboard View**: View the fully automated graphs and P&L statements on the `Command Center` dashboard.

*For Google Sheets copy, follow the link inside the instructions PDF included in the download container.*
