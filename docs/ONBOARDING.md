# Etsy Seller Account Onboarding & Store Configuration Guide

## 1. Pre-Registration Checklist
Before beginning Etsy seller registration, prepare:
- Legal Name & Government ID (Passport / Driver's License)
- Tax / Business Registration Number (GST / EIN / SSN / VAT if applicable)
- Primary Bank Account Details for Payouts
- Mobile Phone with OTP capability

## 2. Store Niche & Name Selection
Recommended Shop Name: **ZenithPlanners Co.** (or **PixelfyStudio**, **OptimaWorkflow**, **PromptPulseStudio**)
*Check name availability on Etsy during registration.*

## 3. Storefront Asset Configuration
- **Shop Logo**: 500x500 px clean, modern vector logomark.
- **Shop Banner**: 1200x300 px (Cover banner) showcasing product categories & brand promise ("Instant Premium Digital Downloads").
- **Announcement**: "Welcome to ZenithPlanners Co. Premium, instant-download digital planners, Notion systems, & creator templates designed to elevate your productivity."
- **Shop Policies**:
  - Digital Delivery: Instant automated download via Etsy purchase confirmation.
  - Returns & Exchanges: Due to the instant digital nature of these products, sales are final. However, customer support guarantees assistance for any download or usage issue within 24 hours.

## 4. Digital Product Delivery Setup
- Set Shipping Profile: **Digital Download** (0 shipping cost, instant fulfillment).
- Automatic PDF customer instruction delivery for every order.
